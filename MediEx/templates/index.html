    How it works

    1. Knowledge acquisition. IF-THEN rules for each disease are derived from a labelled disease-symptom dataset.
    2. Inference engine. Forward chaining: a rule fires when its symptoms are observed. Certainty combines how much of the disease is explained and how much of your input is explained.
    3. History rules. Age and risk factors add small boosts, only to diseases that already match your symptoms.
    4. Explanation facility. Every result shows the rule that fired, matched and missing symptoms, and any history adjustments.
    5. Follow-up questions. The system suggests symptoms that best separate the top candidates.

    About & limits

    The dataset is largely synthetic with many near-duplicate rows, so high accuracy does not prove real clinical performance. Rare diseases, symptom severity over time, lab results and physical examination are not modelled. Nothing you enter is stored by this site.
